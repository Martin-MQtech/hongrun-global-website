Next-Gen Intelligence

Automotive-Grade Predictive Maintenance & Cloud IoT Telemetry

Traditional medical air compressors rely on crude hour-meters that miss real degradation factors. Hongrun incorporates an automotive-grade service forecasting algorithm inspired by modern vehicle ECUs.

Dynamic Wear-Life Prediction (300-Hour Early Warning)

The controller continuously evaluates runtime hours, thermal stress cycles, ambient humidity, and discharge pressure fluctuations to forecast PTFE seal and filter saturation 300 hours prior to critical wear, preventing unplanned clinical downtime.

Real-Time Dew Point Autonomous Purge Guard

Onboard capacitive sensors monitor pressure dew point (≤ -40°C PDP). If moisture spikes are detected, the system autonomously triggers desiccant purge sequencing, shielding expensive turbine handpieces and laboratory spectrometers from moisture contamination.
