Absolute Purity Protocol

Four-Stage Conditioning

Compressed air is only as clean as its conditioning train. Every HYG / HVTG system processes the air through four sequential stages before it reaches your instruments.

01

Bulk Water Separation

Cyclone separator removes condensed moisture and coarse particles immediately after compression — protecting every downstream stage.

02

Desiccant Drying Core

Regenerative desiccant bed drives the dew point down to ≤ -40°C — bone-dry air that never condenses inside handpieces or surgical tools.

03

Particulate HEPA

0.01μm absolute filtration strips dust, rust and desiccant fines — air purity equivalent to Class 1 particulate per ISO 8573-1.

04

Active Carbon (Optional)

Activated carbon stage removes residual odors and volatile organics for cleanrooms, labs and odor-sensitive surgical environments.
