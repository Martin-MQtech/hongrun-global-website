Absolute Purity Protocol

Wet, contaminated air destroys expensive surgical drills and creates biofilm environments within tubing. Our integrated units eliminate this entirely through a rigorous 4-step conditioning process directly out of the pump.

  • Bulk Water Separation: Pre-chillers drop temperature rapidly.
  • Desiccant Drying Core: Twin-tower regenerative desiccant yields -40°C dew point.
  • Particulate HEPA: Traps dust/carbon down to 0.01μm.
  • Active Carbon (Optional): Removes airborne VOCs and hydrocarbons.
