Routine Maintenance Consumables

Medical compression air requires strict ISO 8573-1 Class 0 filtration. The following routine wear items have designated service lives and are excluded from defect warranty once operating hours exceed specified intervals:

  • Air Intake Filter Element (0.01μm) 12 Months / 2,000 Hours
  • Desiccant Molecular Sieve Bed 36 Months / 6,000 Hours
  • Saint-Gobain PTFE Piston Rings Up to 20,000 Hours Lifespan
  • Automatic Drain Valve Solenoid Seal Inspect Every 12 Months

Standard Warranty Exclusions

To maintain ISO 13485 medical device compliance and safety, warranty coverage does not apply under the following conditions:

  • Improper electrical voltage or phase supply outside specified tolerances (±10% from rated VAC/Hz).
  • Operating the compressor in an unventilated enclosed closet where ambient temperatures exceed 40°C (104°F).
  • Unauthorized physical disassembly or modification of pump heads using non-OEM parts.
  • Ingestion of aggressive chemical solvents, chlorine fumes, or abrasive airborne dust into the intake air stream.
