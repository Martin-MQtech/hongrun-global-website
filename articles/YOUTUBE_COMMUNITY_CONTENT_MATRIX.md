🔬 Why "Oil-Less" is NOT Always Medical-Grade: Inside ISO 8573-1 Class 0 Purity 🛡️✨

Did you know that standard industrial "oil-free" air compressors can still deliver aerosolized hydrocarbons, ambient dust particles, and condensed acidic moisture straight to your patients' oral cavities?

ISO 8573-1 Class 0 is a user-specified total-oil quality class, not an absolute-zero or universal medical certification. TÜV Rheinland certificate AK 50528335 0001 / report CN21HFR2 001 documents total oil <0.003 mg/m³ only for complete HW-200 through HW-3600 scroll units.

🏥 What the evidence does—and does not—show:
1. Saint-Gobain self-lubricating PTFE composite seals support lubricant-free compression; friction and service life depend on the selected model and operating conditions
2. Oil-free design does not establish a universal 25,000-hour lifespan or Class 0 status for piston units
3. Receiver hygiene follows the model manual and site plan; antimicrobial or nano-silver performance is not assumed without model-specific evidence
4. A -40°C pressure dew point may be a project requirement/design target; achieved performance requires configuration-specific validation and does not prove sterility
