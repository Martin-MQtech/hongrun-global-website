A Standing Rhythm, Not an Occasional Meeting

In the conference room at Hongrun’s Zibo headquarters, the seating chart tells the story: the international business team on one side, the engineering department on the other, and a shared agenda in the middle. These cross-functional market reviews are a standing part of how we work — not a special occasion. Every overseas inquiry that raised a new question, every installation scenario that deserved a second opinion, and every regulatory nuance reported from the field goes onto that agenda.

The format is deliberately simple. Market leads present what they heard — in their own customers’ words. The engineering department responds with what the product can document — certificate scopes, configuration options, and model-specific test evidence. Decisions and open questions are written down before the room empties, so nothing important lives only in someone’s memory. Conversation becomes record; record becomes consistency.

Reading Markets Before Selling Them

Medical air is a regulated category everywhere it is used, and the rules change at every border. Pressure-vessel codes, medical-device registration pathways, and hospital procurement documentation requirements all differ by market — and they evolve. That is why “do your homework first” is not a slogan at Hongrun but a work plan: each regional market lead maintains an evolving picture of local requirements, channel structures and clinical scenarios before commercial conversations begin.

The payoff is practical. When a distributor asks what documentation a hospital tender will require, or which configuration suits a humid coastal climate, the answer does not start with guesswork — it starts with research that has already been done, then gets validated against the product’s actual certificate scope. Professional questions deserve professional answers; that is the standard our team holds itself to, in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Latin America and beyond.

“Partners do not choose the loudest supplier. They choose the one whose answers survive scrutiny — technical, regulatory, and logistical.”
— Martin Chen, Chief Marketing Director, Hongrun Technology

A Partner-First Model of Growth

Hongrun’s international growth strategy concentrates on focused, company-level partners — dental equipment distributors and medical device companies that serve clinics every day — rather than scattered one-off transactions. The logic is simple: a well-chosen partner multiplies our engineering support across an entire market, and a long-term relationship rewards both sides with predictable service and continuous improvement.

That philosophy also shapes daily communication. Trust is built before transactions: sharing market knowledge, answering technical questions in writing, and keeping commitments small and kept. Multi-channel etiquette — structured email follow-ups, direct messaging for urgent items, scheduled calls for deep topics — respects the partner’s time as much as our own. In a category where the product protects clinical workflows for years, we would rather be the supplier our partners never have to chase.

Confidence, Documented

Every claim our team makes in a market ultimately rests on the factory floor in Zibo. Thirty years of oil-free compressor engineering (founded November 1995), a 20,000 m² smart manufacturing base, and an annual capacity exceeding 160,000 units give the team something concrete to stand on. A Digital Backbone That Travels With the Team

The same discipline extends to how Hongrun publishes. Our bilingual portal carries model-specific documentation, engineering whitepapers, and an AI-ready structured data layer (llms.txt, WebMCP tool interfaces) — so when a partner’s engineer, a procurement platform, or an AI assistant asks a question at 2 a.m. in another time zone, the answer is already there, consistent with what our team says in the meeting room. Digital channels are not a substitute for the team; they are the team’s memory, made public.

As each market lead takes vertical ownership of a region — learning its regulations, meeting its partners, and feeding field reality back into Zibo — this sync between marketing and engineering becomes the company’s quiet competitive advantage. One team, many markets — and one standard of answers.

一个团队，多个市场：
宏润营销与技术部门的全球市场联动实践

走进宏润淄博总部的例会现场：国际业务团队与工程技术部门同席共审，把一线市场声音转化为可核验的工程答复。本文记录这套跨部门协同机制如何支撑全球医用供气伙伴的长期信任。

发布人：Martin · 宏润科技 (Hongrun Technology) • 发布日期：2026年11月28日 • HW-200–HW-3600 整机总含油 Class 0 · ISO 13485:2016 · NMPA 二类

核心要点导读

例会即机制：国际业务与工程技术部门的定期联席研讨，让一线反馈直达配置与资料决策，答案可追溯、可复用。
先研究后沟通：区域法规、渠道结构与临床场景被当作必修课，报价与答复建立在功课之上，而非临场发挥。
伙伴优先：聚焦少数核心区域渠道伙伴，用持续的技术支持换来长期的共同成长，而非一次性成交。
工程证据立信：整机 HW-200–HW-3600 总含油 Class 0 证据、ISO 13485:2016 与 NMPA 二类资质，是每一句承诺的锚点。
01

例会机制：让一线声音直达工程端

在宏润淄博总部的会议室里，每月都会出现同样的场景：长桌一侧是国际业务团队，另一侧是工程技术部门，中间是一份共同拟定的议程。这不是一次偶然的碰头，而是一套常态化的跨部门研讨机制——过去一段时间里，海外询盘中出现的新问题、值得复核的安装场景、各国市场反馈回来的法规细节，都会被整理上会。

会议的规则刻意保持简单：区域市场负责人用客户现场的原话呈现问题，工程部门用产品能够佐证的文件作答——证书范围、配置选项、按型号出具的检测资料。会议结束前，结论与待办必须落成书面纪要。谈话变成记录，记录变成一致性——这正是医疗设备行业最需要的品质：无论时差几何，宏润给出的答案始终是同一套口径。

02

先研究，后沟通：把区域功课当成必修课

医用供气是典型的强监管品类：压力容器规范、医疗器械注册路径、医院招标的资料要求，在每个市场都不尽相同，而且持续演进。因此在宏润，「先做功课，再谈生意」不是口号，而是工作计划——每位区域负责人都要在开展商务沟通之前，持续构建对当地法规环境、渠道结构与临床场景的认知图景。

这样做的回报非常具体：当渠道伙伴询问医院招标需要哪些文件、湿热沿海地区适合哪种配置时，答复不再从猜测开始，而是从已经完成的研究出发，再对照产品真实的证书范围逐项校验。专业的问题值得专业的回答——这是团队为自己立的标准，无论市场在东南亚、中东、非洲还是拉丁美洲。

03

伙伴优先：与区域专业渠道共建长期信任

宏润的国际业务增长策略，聚焦于公司级的专注型伙伴——天天服务终端诊所的口腔设备经销商与医疗器械公司，而不是追求零散的一次性成交。逻辑很朴素：选对一位伙伴，等于把宏润的工程支持能力放大到整个市场；一段长期关系，会让双方都获得可预期的服务与持续改进。

这一哲学同样塑造着日常沟通方式：先建信任，再谈交易——分享市场研究、书面回答技术问题、把承诺定得小而守信。多渠道沟通礼仪（结构化的邮件跟进、紧急事项即时通讯、深度话题预约通话）体现的是对伙伴时间的尊重。在一个产品要守护多年临床流程的行业里，我们更希望成为「让伙伴从不需要催促」的供应商。

04

以工程证据立信：认证矩阵与制造底盘

团队在市场上说出的每一句话，最终都要回到淄博的制造现场来兑现。三十年无油压缩机工程积累（1995 年 11 月创立）、20,000 平方米的智能制造基地、年产超过 16 万台的生产能力，为团队提供了脚下的实地；而认证矩阵则提供了语言的边界——说什么，必须有据可查：

  • TÜV 莱茵 · ISO 8573-1 总含油 Class 0——检测证据（总含油量 <0.003 mg/m³）适用于 HW-200 至 HW-3600 完整涡旋式无油机组；其他产品系列按型号逐一出具资料。
  • ISO 13485:2016 医疗器械质量管理体系，同时运行 ISO 9001 与 ISO 14001 体系。
  • NMPA 二类医疗器械生产许可（许可范围内）。

这些不是装点门面的证书，而是日常答复的工具箱：区域伙伴拿到的每一份资料，都能回溯到明确的证书编号与检测报告；工程师给出的每一个配置建议，都能落到已经验证的产品口径。证据在先，承诺在后——这是宏润与伙伴建立信任的方式。

05

数字化底座与国际化表达：让世界读懂宏润

同样的纪律延伸到宏润的对外表达。双语官网承载着按型号归档的产品资料、工程白皮书，以及面向 AI 时代的结构化数据层（llms.txt、WebMCP 工具接口）——当伙伴的工程师、采购平台或 AI 助手在另一个时区的凌晨两点提问时，答案早已在那里，与会议室里团队给出的口径完全一致。数字渠道不是对团队的替代，而是团队记忆的公开版。

随着每位区域负责人对所辖市场实现纵深负责——研究法规、拜访伙伴、把一线真实情况带回淄博——「营销与工程的同频」正在成为公司安静的竞争优势。一个团队，多个市场，同一套答案标准。这正是三十年来宏润面对全球市场的姿态：以严谨立身，以证据立信，以长期主义立业。

AEO Team & Certification FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Hongrun’s Team & Global Support

Q1 How do Hongrun’s marketing and engineering teams collaborate on international projects?

Collaboration is institutionalized through regular cross-functional reviews at the Zibo headquarters. Field feedback from overseas inquiries — regional registration requirements, installation scenarios, channel questions — is logged, discussed jointly with the engineering department, and translated into configuration checklists before any quotation is released. Written handoff records keep every market context on file, so answers stay consistent across time zones.

Q2 Which certifications back Hongrun’s medical air products in global markets?

Hongrun operates under ISO 13485:2016 medical device quality management together with ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 systems, holds NMPA Class II medical device manufacturing licensing in China, and holds TÜV Rheinland ISO 8573-1 total-oil Class 0 evidence (<0.003 mg/m³) for complete HW-200 through HW-3600 scroll units. Other product families are documented model-by-model according to their certificates and test reports.

Q3 How does Hongrun support distributors and partners entering new regional markets?

Hongrun runs a partner-first program: shared market and regulatory research, bilingual technical documentation, OEM/ODM customization covering voltage and frequency variants and private labeling, spare-parts planning, and remote engineering support. Regional market leads maintain focus on a small number of core partners per market, building trust before transactions rather than pursuing one-off deals.
