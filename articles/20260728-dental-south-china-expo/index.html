Hongrun Technology Shines at the 32nd Dental South China International Expo

March 24, 2025 · 5 min read · Guangzhou International Convention Center

Figure 1.0: Live dynamic operating demonstrations at Hongrun Exhibition Booth, Hall 15.2.

At the 32nd Dental South China International Expo in Guangzhou, Hongrun Compressor Technology Co., Ltd. presented its full lineup of NMPA Class II certified medical gas products, highlighted by the debut of the HYG-IoT Next-Gen Intelligent Clean Air Station.

Over four exhibition days, Hongrun welcomed more than 850 overseas visitors and regional dental supply distributors from Germany, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Brazil, and Southeast Asia. Live acoustic monitoring demonstrated operating noise levels below 52 dB(A), drawing widespread praise from clinical directors.
