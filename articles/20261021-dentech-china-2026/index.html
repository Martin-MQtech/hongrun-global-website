The 8 Core Subsystems of the Modern Dental Plant Room

Traditional dental clinics frequently encounter crippling operational friction: micro-oil leaks from non-certified compressors gumming up multimillion-dollar ceramic milling spindles and handpiece bearings; severe pneumatic pressure drops when multiple treatment chairs operate high-speed handpieces concurrently; acoustic resonance echoing into patient treatment operatories; and foul vacuum exhaust odors recirculating through clinical hallways.

At DenTech China 2026, Hongrun Technology presents the definitive, architecturally unified answer to these systemic vulnerabilities through its 8 Core Dental Equipment Room Subsystems:

Medical Oil-Free Compressor (Pure Air Source)

Specialized biological pipeline cleaning solution. Rapidly disintegrates microscopic bacterial biofilms inside suction tubing, negative pressure headers, and dental chair drainage channels while remaining completely non-corrosive to copper, stainless steel, and medical polymers.

Maintenance: Biofilm Neutralization · Anti-Corrosion

02

重磅发布：口腔机房设备数智化整体解决方案

作为现代口腔诊所与大型口腔医院运转的“动力心脏”，机房设备的稳定性、气体洁净度与感控防护水平，直接关系到高速手机寿命、种植手术成功率、感控达标以及诊疗环境品质。传统模式下，空压机、抽吸机、纯水机、污水机各自采购，管路缺乏统筹规划，常常面临“噪音扰民、压降剧烈、微油微水损坏牙椅手机、负压抽吸交叉污染、故障排查难”五大痛点。

在本届上海口腔展上，宏润科技在 2号馆 M47-48, M69-70 展位 正式重磅推出全新的“口腔机房设备数智化整体解决方案”。秉承“安全节能 · 降本增效 · 精益管理”的核心理念，宏润打破单一单机销售思维，将现代口腔机房重构为高效协同的“数智有机体”：

01. 牙科电动无油空压机 —— 洁净气源

严格执行国家《医用气体工程技术规范》GB 50751-2012标准，核心涡旋主机通过 ISO 8573-1 Class 0 零级无油认证，搭配双塔吸干机实现 -40°C 深度压力露点，杜绝冷凝水与油污残留。

02. 牙科电动无油空压机 —— 静音涡旋空压机

开放式裸机实测噪音稳定在 ≤65 dB(A)；针对室内紧凑型机房推出的加装声学降噪箱款（Acoustic Canopy）更进一步实现 58 dB(A) 极度静音，确保诊疗区与候诊区宁静无扰。

03. 牙科电动无油空压机 —— 基础版

针对 1~3 张牙椅门诊精细化定制，小巧紧凑、即插即用、超低功耗，具备多机头冗余备份能力，保障诊所全天候不间断运营。

04. 牙科电动抽吸系统

高真空干式/半干式中央电动抽吸站，搭载智能变频恒压调控与气水分离系统，配备医疗级排气过滤，杜绝气溶胶倒灌与异味溢出。

05. 医疗污水处理系统

诊所专用集成式污水消毒设备，多级精密过滤搭配全自动臭氧/氯化强效灭菌，排放指标完全符合国家医疗污水感控规范与环保验收标准。

06. 医疗纯化水处理系统

双级反渗透（RO）与 EDI 深度脱盐技术，产水电阻率与纯净度稳定，专供灭菌消毒室高压蒸汽灭菌锅及诊疗椅管路，消除水垢与热原。

07. 空气净化消毒器/系统

等离子体低温除菌场与医疗级高效 HEPA 过滤网深度融合，实现人机共存环境下的诊室空气动态持续消杀，截断空气传播感控风险。

08. 季铵盐管路消毒液

专研生物管路除垢配方，强效溶解抽吸管路内侧顽固生物膜（Biofilm）与凝血杂质，对铜管、工程塑料与密封硅胶温和无腐蚀。

DenTech China 2026 & Hongrun Booth FAQ

Quick answers for visitors, clinic engineers, and international distribution partners.

Q1: Where is Hongrun Technology located at DenTech China 2026?

Yes! Senior Hongrun pneumatic and gas application engineers are stationed at Booth M47-48, M69-70 with CAD workstations to review floor plans, calculate chair diversity flow rates, simulate piping pressure drops, and issue equipment room specifications.

Q4: How do Hongrun compressors ensure zero oil contamination in dental air?

Hongrun HW-series medical compressors employ precision oil-free scroll chambers featuring self-lubricating PTFE/polymer composite seal rings. There is zero hydrocarbon oil in the compression chamber, verified by TÜV Rheinland to achieve ISO 8573-1 Class 0 total oil-free air.

Q5: How quiet is the equipment for in-clinic installation?

Standard open bare units operate at ≤65 dB(A). Models outfitted with Hongrun's specialized Acoustic Canopy (声学降噪箱款) achieve a whisper-quiet sound level of 58 dB(A), allowing direct installation in treatment backrooms without acoustic disturbance.
