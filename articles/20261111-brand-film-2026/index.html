1. A Brand Built on Three Decades of Unbroken Resolve

Some companies tell their story in mission statements. Hongrun Compressor Technology tells it in compressed air — 30 years of it, delivered to the dental chair of a village clinic in Yunnan, the mass spectrometer of a national research laboratory, and the sterile operating suite of a Grade-A tertiary hospital.

Founded in 1995 in Zibo, Shandong — the ancient capital of the Qi State — Hongrun was born from a simple but radical conviction: China's healthcare and research institutions deserved their own sovereign supply of zero-oil, medically pure compressed air. At the time, that purity was locked behind imported equipment at prices only the largest hospitals could afford. Founder Mr. Zhang Runguo set out to change that.

2. The 1995 Origin: Birthplace of China's First Oil-Free Compressor

Three decades ago, China's dental clinics and hospital laboratories were wholly dependent on imported oil-free compressors from Europe and Japan, with lead times measured in months and service responsiveness measured in frustration. The critical technology — the wobble-piston oil-free compression mechanism — was proprietary, unshared, and fiercely guarded.

Working from a modest workshop in Zibo, Hongrun's founding engineering team dismantled, analyzed, prototyped, and tested thousands of piston iterations. In 1995, the breakthrough came: China's very first self-developed, fully oil-free air compressor was successfully energized and validated. That historic first machine is preserved today at the Hongrun headquarters — a silent monument to self-reliance.
