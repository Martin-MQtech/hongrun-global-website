1. The Manufacturing Moat: 30 Years of Dedicated Pneumatic Craftsmanship (1995–2025)

Founded in 1995, Shandong Hongrun Compressor Technology Co., Ltd. has dedicated three decades exclusively to advancing precision pneumatic engineering. From an early pioneering workshop to a specialized medical equipment manufacturing powerhouse, Hongrun has remained loyal to one foundational mission: building a world-class air compressor brand that safeguards clinical health and scientific discovery.

Unlike commercial assembly assemblers who rely on generic third-party motor heads, Hongrun operates complete in-house manufacturing loops. Precision five-axis CNC machining centers, automated stator winding lines, and dedicated dynamic balancing rigs ensure that every wobble-piston, eccentric shaft, and valve plate meets aerospace-grade geometric tolerances within ±3 micrometers.

2. Medical Purity Architecture: NMPA Class II Licensing & ISO 8573-1 Class 0 Certification

Medical compressed air interfaces directly with human tissue, surgical wounds, and micro-precision dental handpieces. Operating under rigorous regulatory scrutiny, Hongrun holds official NMPA Class II medical device registration (Class II Hospital & Dental Equipment) and audited compliance under ISO 13485:2016 medical quality management systems.

Furthermore, our compression streams are independently tested and certified to ISO 8573-1 Class 0 zero-oil purity standards by leading third-party inspection bodies including TÜV Rheinland. By utilizing self-lubricating Saint-Gobain Rulon composite PTFE piston seals and completely oil-free crankcases, Hongrun systems physically eliminate the possibility of hydrocarbon aerosol or oil vapor entering patient treatment lines.

3. Smart Manufacturing Scale: 160,000 Units Annually with National Grade-I Energy Efficiency

Spanning modern industrial production facilities in Zibo High-Tech Industrial Development Zone, Hongrun’s smart manufacturing base integrates high-speed CNC machining centers, automated robotic powder coating lines, and computerized acoustic dynamometer testing rooms.

With an annual output capacity of 160,000 smart oil-free compressor units and 40,000 medical suction systems, every unit undergoes rigorous multi-stage end-of-line testing: 100% helium mass-spectrometer leak detection, dielectric voltage breakdown verification, and continuous 4-hour thermal run-in validation under full rated pressure. This uncompromising manufacturing discipline enables Hongrun products to attain China's National Grade-I Energy Efficiency certification.

4. Full-Scenario Healthcare Coverage: From Single Dental Chairs to Centralized Hospital Plants

Hongrun’s modular engineering architecture delivers tailor-made solutions across all scales of healthcare and analytical science:

  • Independent Operatories (1–3 Chairs): Whisper-quiet HY-100 and HY-200 series (≤55 dB(A)) featuring compact under-cabinet form factors.
  • Polyclinics & Specialty Centers (4–8 Chairs): HYTG-300 dual-pump intelligent parallel redundancy stations with cycle-wear balancing and zero clinic downtime.
  • Hospital Medical Gas Stations (15–50+ Chairs/Beds): Centralized HBG and HW multi-scroll systems equipped with PLC touchscreens, IoT cloud monitoring, and -40°C desiccant dryers.

Thirty years of unwavering dedication to precision engineering. Boundless innovation, limitless technology, infinite service. Clean air — for life.

解构年产16万台医疗气源超级工厂：
30年精密制造的“微米级硬核底牌”与智造飞轮

发布人：Martin · 宏润科技 (Hongrun Technology) • 发布日期：2026年09月16日 • ISO 8573-1 Class 0 · NMPA 二类医疗器械资质
01

智造护城河：三十载专精医用气源动力积淀 (1995–2025)

自 1995 年创立以来，山东宏润空压机科技在气体动力领域深耕整整三十年。从早期的开拓探索，到成长为现代化专精特新高新技术企业与二类医疗器械智造基地，宏润始终践行崇高初心：以“宏天下正气 · 润世间万物”为指引，坚定目标打造具有国际竞争力的世界级空气压缩机品牌厂商。

与许多依赖通用采购组装的组装型作坊不同，宏润构建了完整的自主高精密研发与机加工内循环体系。现代化五轴数控加工中心、自动化电机定子绕线产线与严苛的高转速动平衡台架，确保摆动活塞连杆、偏心曲轴与精密阀片的几何加工公差严格控制在 ±3 微米以内。

02

医疗纯度护城河：二类医疗器械注册备案与 ISO 8573-1 Class 0 认证纯度

在口腔临床和外科手术中，压缩空气不仅是机械动力，更直接接触患者组织创面与高精密轴承。宏润科技严谨持有二类医疗器械注册资质（NMPA Class II）与 CE MDR、ISO 13485:2016 国际医疗质量体系认证，产品通过严苛的 ISO 8573-1 Class 0 零级无油纯度检验，从源头杜绝微米级油雾与有害挥发物，构筑坚不可摧的临床安全屏障。

全系医用泵头采用法国圣戈班高纯度复合 PTFE 自润滑活塞环与终身密封耐高温轴承，气路与润滑油物理隔绝，杜绝碳粉脱落与油汽氧化物污染，为种植牙固化、洁牙喷砂及重症手术提供母婴级洁净动力。

03

规模化智能制造：年产 16 万台智能机组与国家一级能效标杆

坐落于淄博国家高新技术产业开发区的宏润现代化智造基地，配备数控加工中心、自动化静电喷涂线与精密声学测试台架。具备年产 16 万台智能无油空压机与 4 万台牙科负压抽吸设备的规模化全球交付能力。

每一台出厂机组均经过 100% 氦质谱气密性复核、耐压绝缘测试及满载 4 小时动态热平衡磨合，并达到国家一级能效技术标杆，兼具超高可靠性、超低能耗与绿色低碳属性。

04

全场景医疗气源覆盖：从单椅门诊到医院级中央供气系统

依托丰富的产品矩阵（包括 HY 系列静音牙科无油机、HVS 抽吸机组及 HBG/HW 医院级集中供气系统），宏润为全球医疗机构与实验室提供全流程定制化解决方案：

  • 独立单椅门诊（1~3台治疗台）： HY-100 与 HY-200 超静音嵌入式机型（≤55 dB(A)），完美融入诊室地柜空间；
  • 中型口腔门诊部（4~8台治疗台）： HYTG-300 双机头智能并联冗余供气机站，自动轮换磨损平衡，保障门诊不停工；
  • 大型口腔医院与综合医疗枢纽（15~50+台治疗台）： 模块化中央气站系统，搭载 PLC 彩色触控屏、物联网云端运维及 -40°C 深度干燥吸附塔。

三十载专精智造，创新无止境、技术无止境、服务无止境。纯净气源，守护生命。
