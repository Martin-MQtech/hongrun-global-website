Dental Operatory Equipment: Engineering Selection & Pneumatic Sizing Reference for 15 Dental Chairs

Hongrun Clinical EPC Application Dept August 22, 2026 8 min technical read

Executive Engineering Summary: 15-Chair Pneumatics Standard

For a modern 15-chair stomatological clinic or regional dental center, continuous chair downtime is unacceptable. Using standard international simultaneous utilization coefficient k = 0.70, the required compressed air supply is 1,050 L/min @ 0.8 MPa, and central vacuum suction requirement is 4,500 L/min air-flow with -18 to -32 kPa dynamic operating suction. A dual-redundant multi-head architecture is mandatory to eliminate single-point operational failures.

1. Mathematical Formula for Flow Sizing (Simultaneous Coefficient k = 0.70)

In clinic design, sizing pneumatic delivery by simple arithmetic multiplication (15 × 100 L/min = 1,500 L/min) results in oversized, inefficient equipment. Empirical hospital engineering applies the simultaneous utilization curve:

Q_total = N × q_single × k_simultaneous × S_safety
Q_total = 15 chairs × 100 L/min × 0.70 × 1.0 = 1,050 L/min @ 0.8 MPa (8.0 bar)

To deliver 1,050 L/min with 100% duty cycle stability, the clinic requires either a single HYT-1200 (1,200 L/min, 9.0 kW) unit or a dual-unit redundant skid consisting of two HYT-500 (600 L/min each) units.
