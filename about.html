Our Origin

A Mission Beyond a Company
A Nation's Drive for Precision

Scaling the Summit of Precision

From one veteran's workshop to a globally trusted precision enterprise

In 1995, a military veteran stood in a workshop in Zibo, Shandong — the ancient heart of Chinese industry. He carried more than a business plan; he carried a conviction that China's manufacturing future belonged not in mass production, but in precision engineering worthy of global trust.

Three decades of relentless accumulation followed. Each generation of engineers inherited not just the machines, but the mission: to push Chinese manufacturing into the highest tiers of precision instrument and advanced medical engineering. What began as one veteran's workshop is now a 160,000-unit-per-year enterprise — yet the founding conviction remains unchanged.

Today, Hongrun's systems serve the world's most demanding customers — Agilent, Bruker, Shimadzu, 190+ research institutes including Tsinghua and Peking University. Every unit carries the weight of that original expectation: that Chinese precision can stand shoulder to shoulder with the world's best.

Company Timeline

1995 — 2026: Milestones

1995

Company Founded

Military veteran establishes Hongrun in Zibo, Shandong. First oil-free dental compressor produced. Core mission: "Honest, lasting engineering."

2002

First OEM Partnership

Secured first major OEM supply contract for dental compressors. Production capacity expanded to 10,000 units annually.

2008

ISO Certification & Hospital Entry

Achieved ISO 9001 quality management certification. Launched first hospital-grade central gas system — serving major dental hospitals in Shandong Province.

2012

ISO 13485 Medical Device Quality

Achieved ISO 13485 medical device quality management. NMPA Class I medical device registration. Products available nationwide through authorized distributors.

2016

Class II Medical Device License

Hongrun became one of the very few dental compressor manufacturers in China to hold NMPA Class II Medical Device registration — qualifying for life-support hospital central gas systems.

2018-2019

National Technology SME & Shandong Famous Brand

Certified National Technology-based SME and Shandong Provincial Specialized & Sophisticated SME. Awarded Shandong High-quality Brand, Governor Cup Industrial Design Excellence, and Qilu Cup prizes.

2022-2023

Shandong Famous Brand & Postdoctoral Innovation Base

Honored as Shandong Famous Brand. Approved as Shandong Postdoctoral Innovation Practice Base — building a high-end talent research platform for next-generation medical gas technology.

2021

专精特新 "Little Giant" Designation

Designated a 专精特新 "Specialized & Sophisticated" enterprise by Shandong Province — China's recognition for innovation-leading SMEs. Grade-I Energy Efficiency certification achieved.

2024

160,000 Units / Year Capacity

New manufacturing hall commissioned. Total production capacity reaches 160,000 units/year including 40,000 dental-specific units. 65,000+ active clinic installations worldwide.

2026

30+ Years Strong — Going Global

Celebrating 30+ years of honest engineering. Launching international dealer network expansion in EU, Southeast Asia, and Middle East markets. Same values. Same quality. New frontiers.
