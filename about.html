Our Origin

Built on Military Discipline, Driven by a Daughter's Ambition

Hongrun Compressor Technology was founded in 1995 by a military veteran in Zibo, Shandong — a man who believed that true engineering meant uncompromising reliability, not impressive-sounding specifications. His singular mission: equip every clinic and laboratory in China with clean, safe, dependable air.

Three decades later, the same uncompromising integrity lives on — now carried forward by his daughter, who inherited not just the business, but the philosophy. Under her leadership, Hongrun evolved from a regional workshop into a globally trusted OEM partner, exporting to Europe, Southeast Asia, and the Americas.

Today our state-of-the-art facility in Zibo produces 160,000+ units annually, supplying top-tier dental hospitals, precision instrument analytics labs (Agilent, Bruker, Shimadzu), and 190+ research institutes — all backed by the most demanding regulatory certifications in the industry.

Company Timeline

1995 — 2026: Milestones

1995

Company Founded

Military veteran establishes Hongrun in Zibo, Shandong. First oil-free dental compressor produced. Core mission: "Honest, lasting engineering."

2002

First OEM Partnership

Secured first major OEM supply contract for dental compressors. Production capacity expanded to 10,000 units annually.

2008

ISO Certification & Hospital Entry

Achieved ISO 9001 quality management certification. Launched first hospital-grade central gas system — serving major dental hospitals in Shandong Province.

2012

ISO 13485 Medical Device Quality

Achieved ISO 13485 medical device quality management. NMPA Class I medical device registration. Products available nationwide through authorized distributors.

2016

Class II Medical Device License

Hongrun became one of the very few dental compressor manufacturers in China to hold NMPA Class II Medical Device registration — qualifying for life-support hospital central gas systems.

2019

ISO 8573-1 Class 0 & CE Certification

Achieved the highest possible compressed air purity standard — ISO 8573-1 Class 0 (zero detectable oil). CE marking opens European Union markets. Annual capacity reaches 80,000 units.

2021

专精特新 "Little Giant" Designation

Designated a 专精特新 "Specialized & Sophisticated" enterprise by Shandong Province — China's recognition for innovation-leading SMEs. Grade-I Energy Efficiency certification achieved.

2024

160,000 Units / Year Capacity

New manufacturing hall commissioned. Total production capacity reaches 160,000 units/year including 40,000 dental-specific units. 65,000+ active clinic installations worldwide.

2026

Celebrating 30 years of honest engineering. Launching international dealer network expansion in EU, Southeast Asia, and Middle East markets. Same values. Same quality. New frontiers.
