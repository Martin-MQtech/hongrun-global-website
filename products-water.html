Multi-Stage Purification Topology

6-Barrier Clinical Water Safety System

A configurable multi-stage treatment train for the supplied model. Final water quality and any biofilm-control performance require site-specific commissioning and validation.

01

Sand / Quartz

Reduces suspended solids; filtration rating depends on the selected pre-treatment stage.

02

Active Carbon

Adsorbs residual chlorine, organic matter, odors, and VOCs.

03

Ion Softener

Reduces hardness ions to limit RO scaling; outlet hardness is monitored per the project plan.

04

RO Membranes

Membrane type and rejection performance are stated for the supplied model and feed-water conditions.

05

Ozone Gen

Where configured, ozone dosing and contact conditions are defined by the project disinfection plan.

06

UV-C 254nm

Where configured, UV lamp type, dose and monitoring are stated for the supplied unit.
