Hongrun 30th Anniversary Quality Tour – Part 1: Jinan & Shandong Medical Hub

Published: February 1, 2025 · Shandong Provincial Medical Equipment Base

The inaugural stage of Hongrun's nationwide 30th Anniversary Quality Tour kicked off across Jinan and the surrounding medical manufacturing corridor, auditing over 40 central hospital compressed air stations and OEM assembly plants.

Engineers performed continuous 72-hour thermal stability tests, wear measurement on Saint-Gobain PTFE composite piston rings exceeding 20,000 continuous operating hours, and comprehensive pneumatic leak diagnostics.
