Clinical Engineering FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Compressed Air

Engineering specifications, sizing calculations, and ISO Class 0 compliance data for clinic operators and hospital engineers.

1 What size air compressor do I need for 1, 2, or 3 dental chairs?

For a 1-chair solo practice, the HY-100 (0.55 kW, 100 L/min, 24L tank) or HY-200 (0.75 kW, 150 L/min, 32L tank) provides ample volume. For 2 to 3 chairs, the HY-300 (1.1 kW, 200 L/min, 50L slim tank) delivers 200 L/min at 5 bar, satisfying simultaneous handpiece and 3-way syringe air flow with a 0.70 diversity coefficient.

2 Why is ISO 8573-1 Class 0 certified oil-free air mandatory in dental clinics?

Certified Class 0 oil-free air eliminates oil aerosol contamination that compromises resin-dentin bond strength (reducing shear bond strength by 35% to 60%) and prevents premature failure of high-speed handpiece ceramic turbine bearings. Hongrun compressors achieve measured residual oil content of 0.003 mg/m³, significantly cleaner than the strict 0.01 mg/m³ ISO Class 0 limit.

3 How quiet are Hongrun HY series dental compressors during operatory treatment?

Hongrun HY series compressors operate at ultra-quiet noise levels between 52 dB(A) and 60 dB(A) due to dynamic balancing, acoustic baffling, and double-damped rubber mounts, allowing direct point-of-use installation next to treatment operatories without disturbing patients.

4 Why is dual-pump redundancy recommended for clinics with 4 or more chairs?

For clinics operating 4 to 8 dental chairs, the HYTG-300 features two synchronized ZB300 rocking piston heads on a single 90L antibacterial tank with automated alternating PLC control. If one pump head undergoes scheduled maintenance, the second unit maintains clinical air delivery, preventing costly clinic downtime.

5 What is the expected service life and maintenance schedule of Hongrun rocking piston compressors?

Equipped with imported Saint-Gobain diamond-impregnated PTFE composite piston rings and NSK precision bearings, Hongrun compressor heads achieve a continuous wear life of 20,000 to 25,000 operating hours. Maintenance requires only periodic intake air filter inspection (every 6–12 months) and automatic condensate drain checks.
