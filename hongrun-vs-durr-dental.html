Frequently Asked Comparison Questions

Q1: How does air purity compare between Hongrun and Dürr Dental?

Both manufacturers achieve the highest international purity rating: ISO 8573-1:2010 Class 0 (100% Oil-Free). Hongrun’s purity is certified by TÜV Rheinland with hydrocarbon emissions measured at <0.003 mg/m³—over 3 times cleaner than the strict 0.01 mg/m³ Class 0 limit. Both guarantee zero chemical contamination during composite resin photopolymerization.

Q2: Why does Hongrun recommend dual-pump duplex systems (HYTG-300) over single large units?

A single large compressor is a Single Point of Failure (SPOF); if an electric motor requires maintenance, all clinic chairs halt immediately. The Hongrun HYTG-300 houses twin independent ZB300 rocking-piston pump heads with smart PLC alternating control. In the event of routine maintenance on one head, the companion head maintains 200 L/min (7.1 CFM), enabling priority surgical and hygiene procedures to proceed without clinic closure.

Q3: How are replacement parts and field service supported internationally?

Hongrun maintains a 12-Hour Engineering Triage protocol. Critical warranty and replacement components (PTFE piston rings, reed valves, pressure switches, desiccant modules) are dispatched via DHL/FedEx Express directly from our Zibo automated factory base within 24 to 48 hours, supported by certified regional distribution partners across 50+ countries.
