Turnkey Medical Gas Engineering

Two Supplies, One Suction

A hospital breathing system is three networks engineered as one. Hongrun designs, supplies and commissions all of them — so air, oxygen and vacuum arrive at the bedhead from a single accountable partner.

Central Air Supply

HBG / HW / HWG scroll plants with Class 0 oil-free output, desiccant drying and redundant duty/standby configuration for uninterrupted instrument air.

Central Oxygen

Medical oxygen manifolds, terminal units and pipeline distribution engineered to national medical gas standards — sized to your bed count and critical care profile.

Vacuum Negative Pressure

Central suction plants with bacterial filtration and compliant negative-pressure control — protecting patients, staff and isolation rooms alike.
