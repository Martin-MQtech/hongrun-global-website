Privacy Policy & Data Protection Statement

Effective Date: November 28, 2026 · Shandong Hongrun Air Compressor Technology Co., Ltd.

1. Controller & Scope

Shandong Hongrun Air Compressor Technology Co., Ltd. ("Hongrun", "we", "our") is the controller of business contact information submitted through this website. This policy explains how we handle information provided through RFQ, OEM and catalog forms, direct email or WhatsApp contact, and anonymous website measurement. It applies to hongrun1995.cn and its public subpages.

2. Information We Receive

  • Information you submit: name, business email, country or region, company or clinic, product interests, project specifications, and any other details you choose to include.
  • Direct communications: the content and contact details you send by email or WhatsApp, including attachments.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser and device information, request logs, and cache records needed to deliver and protect the website.

Please do not submit patient records, clinical records, payment-card data, or other information that is not needed for a business inquiry.

3. How We Use Information & Legal Bases

Where applicable data-protection law applies, we process information to respond to your request, assess technical requirements, prepare quotations, manage OEM or distributor discussions, provide after-sales support, maintain security, and meet accounting or other legal obligations. Depending on the purpose, the legal basis may be steps requested before a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate business interests, compliance with law, or your consent. We do not sell inquiry contact details or use them for advertising profiles.

4. Service Providers & International Processing

  • Formspree receives website form submissions and transmits them to our business inquiry mailbox.
  • Cloudflare provides website delivery, caching, and security services and may process request and security logs.
  • Google Analytics measures aggregate site usage only after you select "Accept Analytics". Inquiry names, email addresses, companies, and form messages are not sent to Analytics.
  • Email and WhatsApp providers process communications when you choose those direct contact channels.

These providers may process data in countries other than your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers are subject to applicable legal safeguards. Provider availability and processing locations can change; we review the services we use before making material changes to this policy.

5. Cookies, Analytics & Browser Storage

Google Analytics is not requested before a consent choice. Selecting "Essential Only" keeps analytics denied. Selecting "Accept Analytics" allows analytics cookies and aggregate usage measurement; advertising storage and advertising personalization remain denied. You can withdraw analytics consent by clearing this site's browser storage and choosing "Essential Only" on your next visit.

Your browser stores the consent choice, a submission-throttling timestamp with a non-reversible email hash, and public website content cached by the service worker for offline viewing. Routine inquiry payloads are not stored in plaintext in your browser. Earlier-version plaintext inquiry backups are removed when the consent module runs.

6. Retention & Security

We retain inquiry information only as long as needed for the inquiry, quotation, contractual relationship, technical support, or applicable legal and accounting obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it where practicable. The website uses HTTPS and business access controls; no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your Choices & Rights

Subject to the law applicable to you, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and you may withdraw consent at any time. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where law requires. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Contact & Policy Updates

For privacy questions or data-rights requests, email info@hongrun1995.cn or contact Shandong Hongrun Air Compressor Technology Co., Ltd., National High-Tech Industrial Development Zone, Zibo, Shandong 255000, China. We will update this page if our data practices materially change; the effective date above identifies the current version.
